Written in the quiet hours
My Dearest Miss X,

I have started this letter a dozen times, and torn up every version, because none of them held still long enough to say what I mean. So I will stop trying to arrange the words neatly, and just tell you plainly: you have become the thought I return to between every other thought.

It is a strange thing, how someone can rearrange the shape of your days without ever meaning to. Before you, my mornings were just mornings. Now they are measured in how soon I might hear from you, how soon I might see that particular way you tilt your head when you are about to say something clever.

I notice everything. The way your laugh arrives a half-second before you expect it to. The quiet concentration on your face when you are focused on something that matters to you. The kindness you hand out so easily, as if it costs you nothing, when I know it is one of the most generous things a person can give.

I am not writing to ask you for anything. I only wanted you to know, in your own time, in your own hands, that you are thought of — carefully, warmly, and often. Whatever this becomes, I am glad to know you. I am gladder still to be someone who gets to.

Until I see you again,

Yours
